# Items

Gelli Fields has many items, which can often be categorized into four distinct categories:

- Crops/Fruits
- Seeds
- Critters
- Other

## <img src="../../assets/images/gelli fields/items/Apple.png" width=40 style="float: left; margin-left: 0px;">Crops
<br>
Crops (or fruits) are the products produced by growing seeds and harvesting the crops when they're done growing. Pretty self-explanatory.

## <img src="../../assets/images/gelli fields/items/Apple-Tree-Seeds.png" width=40 style="float: left; margin-left: 0px;"> Seeds
<br>
Seeds are used to plant crops and are often bought from the shop.

## <img src="../../assets/images/gelli fields/items/Blue-Slab-Beetle.png" width=40 style="float: left; margin-left: 0px;"> Critters
<br>
Critters are the item version of any critters (bugs or fish) that are caught. Their only use currently is to be sold.

## <img src="../../assets/images/gelli fields/items/Leaf.png" width=40 style="float: left; margin-left: 0px;"> Other

Other items are just general items you can find such as sticks, stones, and other random things.
